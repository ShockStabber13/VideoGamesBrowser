V3.2 STRICT IGDB DAILY CHUNK RULE
- IGDB-only direct-index rows now require a genuine positive IGDB ID.
- Normal Daily Chunk builds reuse proven cached title mappings and try exact-title IGDB resolution for missing curated mappings when IGDB credentials are available.
