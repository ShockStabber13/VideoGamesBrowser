# VideoGamesBrowser curated quality review

Featured membership is a deliberate cross-platform editorial selection, not a rating leaderboard.
Do not grow it to meet a numerical target. Validate every Steam AppID and exact IGDB/platform identity.
The separate quality-review.json documents only reviewed removals; current baseline game IDs remain authoritative.

Daily Chunks must have an actionable, realistically finishable play-session goal specific to the gameplay.
Game-specific curated instructions take precedence over old franchise templates. The rules in quality-review.json
fix explicitly reviewed title/franchise mismatches such as Sonic racing games inheriting Sonic platformer objectives,
Pokémon Snap inheriting RPG gym/training instructions, and on-rails Resident Evil games inheriting survival-horror
typewriter instructions. Unmatched legacy franchise rules stay untouched until reviewed. No synthetic game IDs
or genre filler are generated.

Both workflows download the current release first and validate IDs against the IGDB/platform membership index
and the strict IGDB+DAT catalog. The publishing output retains only IDs, minimal sort/filter keys,
Featured membership and the carefully reviewed Daily Chunk goals; rich metadata remains live in the Android app.
